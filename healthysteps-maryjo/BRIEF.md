## Decisions
- One page, no newsletter signup, no blog. Her old nav had ~10 pages; the one-pager keeps
  classes, program, bio, testimonials, photos, contact, and one press link.
- Shop page (WooCommerce: free demo / $12 drop-in / $50 monthly) is folded into the Zoom
  class card as prices + "Email for a Zoom invite". No cart on the demo.
- Stale class dates (Aug–Oct 2026 sessions) are dropped; cards say "call or email for dates".
- Copy is lightly rewritten from her own pages in plain, short sentences.
