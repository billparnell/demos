What is Healthy Steps?

Exercise that started as a gift to a mom

Sherry Lebed Davis and her doctor brothers created Healthy Steps to help their mother recover from breast cancer treatment. They mixed exercises for lymphedema with gentle dance moves to lift her mood and energy. It worked, and today it's taught in about 900 places around the world.

Every class starts with lymphatic breathing, then moves through easy routines set to upbeat music. There's always time to talk and laugh.

Learn more at gohealthysteps.com

  • 1Starts where you are. Safe for people dealing with cancer, diabetes, knee or back pain, or recovering from surgery.
  • 2Better balance and strength for everyday things: stairs, jars, getting up from a chair.
  • 3More energy and easier movement, a little more each week.
  • 4Friends who show up for you. Many students say the group feels like family.
Meet your teacher

Hi, I'm Mary Jo

I started dancing at age 5 and plan to never stop. I taught in public schools for 38 years, including 20 years with high school students facing emotional challenges.

In 2006 a friend told me about Healthy Steps, and I trained with its founder, Sherry Lebed Davis. I've taught it in Chico ever since: at Enloe Cancer Center, through Chico Rec, and on Zoom with students from all over the country.

My cat, Custard Tart, sometimes joins the Zoom classes.
