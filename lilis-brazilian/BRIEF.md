# Lili's Brazilian Steakhouse — homepage redesign mockup

## What this is
A homepage mockup for **Lili's Brazilian Steakhouse** in Chico, CA. The restaurant's
current site is https://lilisbraziliansteakhouse.com (built by DK Web Design). The
old name was "Lili's Brazilian Bistro", and lilisbrazilianbistro.com redirects to the new site.

This is a design mockup only: no app, no deploy. It lives as a Claude design canvas:
**https://claude.ai/artifact/5qMarACV41Eytoq6MV2RfM**. The link is private until it
is shared from the page's Share menu.

## The brief (Bill, 2026-11-18)
> Mock up a homepage using the current logo but make something a little less regal
> and more vibrant… something that really embraces a Brazilian vibe.

## Facts pulled from the live site (as of 2026-11-18)
- 2760 Esplanade, Suite 100, Chico, CA 95973 · (530) 399-0675 · lilisbraziliansteakhouse@gmail.com
- Hours: Mon closed · Tue–Thu 4:30–9pm · Fri–Sat 11am–10pm · Sun 11am–2pm (brunch)
- The site's nav: Home, Reservations, Order Takeout, Brazilian Steakhouse, Private Events, Meet Lili, Contact
- Dishes named on the site: Chicken Strogonoff, Picanha, Vaca Atolada, Feijoada, natural juices, Brazilian coffee
- Testimonials used are the three on the live homepage (Hesam A., Lori M., Brandon D.). They are lightly trimmed.

## Decisions
- **Keep the logo untouched.** The brief said to use the current logo. The gold flame logo
  reads fine on deep green, so the redesign changes everything around it instead.
- **Reuse their own copy and photos** wherever possible, so the mockup reads as *their*
  site and not a stock template.
